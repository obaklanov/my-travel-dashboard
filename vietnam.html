Why Vietnam?

Vietnam is an incredible destination because it effortlessly pairs breathtaking natural wonders, like the limestone karsts of Ha Long Bay, with an exceptionally rich history and vibrant cities. On top of its stunning landscapes, it offers one of the world's most celebrated street food cultures and deeply welcoming hospitality, all while remaining highly affordable for travelers.

Places to Explore

Da Nang

Da Nang is a vibrant coastal city in central Vietnam renowned for its sandy beaches, French colonial history, and proximity to the striking Marble Mountains and the iconic Dragon Bridge.

Hoi An

Hoi An is a beautifully preserved historic town in central Vietnam, famous for its atmospheric, lantern-lit Old Quarter, ancient yellow merchants' houses, and rich culinary scene.

Hanoi

Hanoi is the chaotic yet charming capital of Vietnam, beloved for its 1,000-year-old history, a maze-like Old Quarter, Parisian-style colonial architecture, and serene city lakes.

Travel Tips

  • Use Grab: Download the Grab Vietnam app to book safe, fixed-price car and motorcycle rides without haggling.
  • Carry Cash: Keep Vietnamese Đồng (VND) on hand, as street vendors, markets, and small cafes rarely accept cards.
  • Drink Safely: Stick strictly to bottled water and avoid ice at smaller street stalls to prevent stomach bugs.
